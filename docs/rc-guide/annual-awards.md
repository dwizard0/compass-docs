# Annual Awards (RC)

Each year STARFLEET runs its Annual Awards — Member of the Year, Junior Member of the Year, Chapter of the Year and the rest. Nominations start in the chapters, and each region chooses **one winner per category** to go forward to the fleet. This page covers the region's part.

---

## Who does what

| Step | Who |
|---|---|
| Nominate | Any member, and regional staff |
| Choose each chapter's nominees | The chapter's CO or XO |
| Review nominations for the region | RAO |
| Choose the region's winners and submit them to Fleet | RC, VRC |
| Record Fleet's decision | RC, VRC |

---

## The annual awards year

When the fleet opens Annual Awards nominations, a **🏆 Annual Awards *year* — Nominations Open** notice appears under **Attention Required** on your [Regional Dashboard](regional-dashboard.md), with the fleet's target date. Plan your region's review so your winners are submitted before then.

---

## Where you work

All of the steps below happen on **Awards → Nominations**, in the **Annual Awards** section. It has a tab for each stage you're involved in, each with a count of what's waiting:

- **My Nominations** — nominations you submitted yourself.
- **RAO Review** — for the RAO.
- **RC Selection** — for the RC and VRC.
- **Fleet Submission** — for the RC and VRC.

If more than one year has nominations, choose the year at the top.

---

## How nominations reach the region

1. Members nominate during the nomination period.
2. Each chapter's CO or XO chooses **one nomination per category** for their chapter, and forwards the selected ones to the region.
3. Forwarded nominations go to the **RAO**. If your region has no RAO in COMPASS, they go straight to **RC Selection**.

---

## RAO Review

The RAO reviews each nomination the chapters forwarded.

- **✓ Approve** — it goes on to RC Selection.
- **Reject** — it goes no further. A reason is required and is kept with the nomination.

Click **View Full Nomination** to read the whole nomination, including the basis for the recommendation.

---

## RC Selection

The RC (or VRC) picks the region's winner in each category.

1. Open the **RC Selection** tab. Approved nominations are grouped by category.
2. Read the nominations in a category, then click **✓ Select as Winner** on one. **One winner per category** represents your region at fleet level.

Changed your mind? Selecting a different nomination in the same category replaces your earlier choice, or click **Unselect** on the winner. Either way, you can only change it until you've submitted it to Fleet.

### The region award

Each fleet award has a regional edition, so your winner also receives the region-level award when you select them.

The nominator chose whether it should be announced straight away or held for a presentation. A held award shows **⏳ Region Award Pending — *event name*** until it's presented. After the ceremony, click **Mark Presented** (here, or on **Awards → Pending Presentations**) to add the award to the member's record.

---

## Fleet Submission

Your winners appear on the **Fleet Submission** tab.

For each winner:

- **⬇ Download PDF Form** — a pre-filled Annual Award Nomination Form.
- **Open SFI Form ↗** and **📋 Copy** — to submit through SFI's own form, copying the details across.
- **✓ Mark Submitted** — sends the nomination to the fleet **Annual Awards Director** for Executive Committee (EC) evaluation. It then shows **Awaiting EC Evaluation**.

**View Fleet Annual Awards Dashboard** shows where your submissions stand in the fleet's process.

### Recording Fleet's decision

When the fleet announces the results:

- **★ Awarded** — adds the fleet award to the winner's record.
- **✗ Not Awarded** — records that they weren't selected at fleet level. They keep their region award.

**Fleet Decisions (Recent)** at the bottom of the tab lists the outcomes you've recorded.

---

## If your region has no RC in COMPASS

When the RAO approves a nomination and there's no RC in COMPASS to select winners, COMPASS asks for the RC's email address. Enter it and click **📧 Send**: the RC receives a secure link to review the nomination without a COMPASS account.

- The nomination shows **Awaiting** while the link is open, and the days remaining.
- When the RC responds, it shows **✓ Endorsed by *name*** or **✗ Declined by *name***.
- If the link expires with no response, you can **Resend** it, or choose **Process without external RC →** to continue.

---

## Tips

- Agree with your RAO how you'll compare nominations before the review starts, so each category is judged the same way.
- Select and submit well before the fleet's target date; the EC needs time to evaluate.
- Use the downloaded PDF to check a nomination reads well before you mark it submitted.
