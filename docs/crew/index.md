# Crew Member Guide

This guide is for crew members — anyone who is not the CO or XO of their ship. It covers what you see when you log in, how to keep track of your own service record, and how to take part in your ship's events.

!!! note "About the screenshots"
    The screenshots in this guide come from a test copy of COMPASS. Some personal details are blurred, and the numbers on your own pages will of course be different.

---

## Getting Your Account

You don't sign up for COMPASS yourself. When your CO adds your ship's roster to COMPASS, you get a **"Welcome to COMPASS — Your Account is Ready"** email. It lists your SCC, ship, rank and username, and has a **Set Your Password** button.

- The link in that email expires after **24 hours**. If it has expired, use **Forgot password?** on the login page to get a new one.
- If you never received a welcome email, check your spam folder, then ask your CO. Your CO can confirm you're on the roster.

## Logging In

Go to [compass.sfi.org](https://compass.sfi.org) and sign in with your **username or email** and your COMPASS password.

![COMPASS login page](../img/Crew Guide - Login.png)

Your username is the same as on **db.sfi.org**, but your password is separate — changing one does not change the other.

Tick **Remember me** on a device you use often so you stay signed in.

!!! tip "Install COMPASS as an app"
    COMPASS can be installed on your phone or computer like an app. Go to **Profile → Install COMPASS App**, or use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" option. Installing it is also what lets you receive push notifications.

## Finding Your Way Around

The menu on the left is grouped into sections. As a crew member you will usually see:

| Section | Items |
|---|---|
| **Dashboards** | **Crew Dashboard**, **Departments** |
| **Operations** | **Ship Positions**, **Events**, and **Voting** if your chapter uses it |
| **Records** | **Awards**, **Academy**, **Permanent Points** |
| **Account** | **My Record**, **Profile**, **Help / Wiki**, **Logout** |

The **Notifications** bell at the top of the menu shows a count when you have something new.

If you hold a position on your ship or in your region, you'll see extra items for that role. Those are covered in the [Commanding Officer](../co-guide/index.md) and [Regional Coordinator](../rc-guide/index.md) guides.

## Your Dashboard

After logging in you land on the **Crew Dashboard**, a summary of where you stand.

![Crew Dashboard](../img/Crew Guide - Dashboard.png)

From top to bottom:

- **Service Record** — your rank (and since when), position, time in grade, time in service, training badges (OTS, OCC, SFDPP, course count) and your departments.
- **Ship Overview** — active crew, events in the last 12 months, upcoming events and service hours for your ship.
- **Announcements** — messages from your command team, region or the fleet.
- **Notifications** — recent items for you, such as an award you received.
- **Awards & Commendations** — your awards, newest first. Click **Show All** to see the full list.
- **Promotion Progress** — your next rank and how close you are, with checks for time in grade, points and course prerequisites.
- **Points Breakdown** — event and academy points, award points, permanent points and your total.
- **Your Attendance** — events attended, uniforms worn, and your recent events with the points each one earned.
- **Upcoming Events** — your ship's next events.
- **Quick Links** — links your CO has set up, such as a reading challenge form or your ship's website.

## My Record

**My Record** (under Account) is your complete service record in one place.

![My Record](../img/Crew Guide - My Record.png)

It shows:

- **Points Breakdown** — the same totals as your dashboard. If points were spent on a promotion, that is shown under Event + Academy Points.
- **Service History** — every ship you've served on, with dates.
- **Rank Progression** — a timeline of your promotions, oldest on the left. "Start" is the rank you held before the earliest promotion recorded in COMPASS.
- **Academy Courses** — how many courses you've passed with Distinction, Honors and Pass.
- **Recent Event Participation** — your last ten events, whether you were in uniform, and the points each earned. Click **View All** for the full list.
- **Service Stats** — events attended, uniform compliance and time in service.
- **Training Status** — OTS, OCC and SFDPP, with completion dates.
- **Positions** and **Departments** — what you hold on your ship and in your region.
- **Membership** — whether your SFI membership is active and when it expires.
- **Promotion Progress** and **Events by Type**.

Buttons at the top right:

- **Service Record (PDF)** — downloads a printable copy of your record. It includes your promotions, service, positions, awards, training, events and permanent points. It deliberately leaves out your date of birth, address, phone number and email, so it's safe to share.
- **Sync My SFI Data** — signs in to db.sfi.org with your SFI credentials and pulls in your membership expiration, OTS/OCC and course completions. Your SFI password is used once and never stored.
- **Transfer History** and **Leave Ship** — see [Changing Ships](#changing-ships) below.

## Promotion Progress

Your **Promotion Progress** card (on the dashboard and on My Record) shows the next rank you can be promoted to and three checks:

- **TIG** — whether you've served enough time in your current grade
- **Points** — whether you have the points the next rank requires
- **Course Prereqs** — whether you've completed the required Academy courses

When all three are green you are eligible. Promotions on a ship are put forward by your command team, so if you think you're eligible, talk to your CO.

Not sure where the numbers come from? See [How Promotion Points Work](../reference/promotion-points.md) for the point value of every event type, academy grade, award, and permanent point award, plus the point cost and time in grade for each rank.

!!! note "Not assigned to a ship?"
    Members without a ship request promotions themselves. See [Requesting a Promotion (Unassigned)](unassigned-promotion.md).

## Events and RSVPs

Go to **Events** to see your ship's calendar. Switch between **List** and **Calendar** view at the top right.

Open an event to see the details — date and time (shown in your time zone), location, and type.

![Event page](../img/Crew Guide - Event.png)

### RSVP

Events that count for attendance have a **Will you attend this event?** section. Choose **Attending**, **Maybe** or **Not Attending**. You can change your answer until RSVP closes, 24 hours after the event starts.

![RSVP](../img/Crew Guide - RSVP.png)

If you're part of a **family membership**, your family members are listed below you and you can RSVP for each of them.

Your RSVP tells your command team who to expect. It is **not** your attendance — attendance (and your points) is recorded by your command team after the event.

If an event you RSVP'd **Attending** or **Maybe** to is moved, changes location, or is cancelled, COMPASS notifies you automatically.

### Subscribe to your ship's calendar

Click **Subscribe to Calendar** on the Events page to add your ship's events to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. The calendar includes your ship's approved events and updates itself as new ones are approved. If the window says the feed is disabled or not yet available, ask your CO or XO to turn it on.

### Suggesting an event

Crew members can create events too. Click **Create Event** and fill in the form — see [Events](../co-guide/events.md) for what each field means. Your event is submitted as **pending** and appears on the calendar once your command team approves it.

## Awards and Nominations

Go to **Awards** to see the awards you've received.

To recognise a shipmate, go to **Awards → Nominate**.

![Nominate an award](../img/Crew Guide - Nominate.png)

1. Search for the **Nominee** by name or SCC. If they don't have a COMPASS account, tick **Nominee is not in COMPASS**.
2. Choose an **Award Type** to narrow the list, then pick the **Award**.
3. Write a **Citation** of at least 50 characters explaining why they deserve it. Add any **Supporting Evidence** you have.
4. Submit. Your nomination goes to your CO for review before moving on to regional or fleet approval.

See the [Awards guide](../co-guide/awards.md) for advice on writing a strong citation.

## Academy and Permanent Points

**Academy** shows SFI Academy course completions for your ship, including how many courses each member has passed with Distinction, Honors or Pass. Your own course counts also appear on My Record.

**Permanent Points** shows your permanent points — awards, leadership positions, OTS/OCC completions, and other sources — grouped by category.

![Permanent points by category](../img/Crew Guide - Permanent Points.png)

Permanent points never reset and are never spent on a promotion. See [How Promotion Points Work](../reference/promotion-points.md#permanent-points) for the values and the per-category caps.

!!! info "Why doesn't my total add up?"
    Each category has a **cap**. Points awarded above the cap are kept on your record but don't count toward your total. The bar under each category shows how much of the cap you've used; "Cap reached" means extra points in that category won't raise your total.

## Your Profile and Notifications

Go to **Profile** to manage your account.

![Profile](../img/Crew Guide - Profile.png)

- **SFI Identity** — your name, SCC, username, member-since date, membership expiration and status. This is **read only**: it comes from the SFI member database. To change your name or contact details, update them on [db.sfi.org](https://db.sfi.org); they reach COMPASS the next time your command team imports the roster.
- **Change Password** — your COMPASS password.
- **Email Notification Preferences** — turn individual emails on or off:
    - **Award Received**
    - **Promotion Status**
    - **Event Changes** — when an event you RSVP'd to is moved or cancelled
    - **Membership Expiring**
    - **Weekly Summary Email** — one email every Monday rolling up the past week. Nothing is sent for a quiet week.
    - **Pending Approvals** — only shown to members with a command role
- **Timezone** — COMPASS shows times in your ship's time zone. Pick another one here if you live somewhere else.
- **Discord Notifications** — if your account is linked to Discord, you can also get these notices as Discord DMs.
- **Push Notifications** — click **Enable on this Device** to get instant notifications on that phone or computer. Do this on each device you use.
- **Install COMPASS App** — adds COMPASS to your home screen.
- **Notification History** — every notice COMPASS has sent you. You can also reach it from the **Notifications** bell.
- **Ship Assignment** — your current ship, with a **Leave Ship** button.

## Changing Ships

Under SFI policy you can move to another chapter at any time, for any reason. Your service history, awards, academy records, permanent points and rank all come with you.

Click **Leave Ship** (on My Record or your Profile) and choose:

- **Transfer to another ship** — pick another COMPASS ship from the ship browser.
- **Become unassigned** — leave without joining another ship. You stay a STARFLEET member in your region and can join a ship later.
- **Transfer to a chapter not on COMPASS** — optionally type the chapter's name.

Your chapter is officially set on **db.sfi.org**, and COMPASS follows it. After you choose, COMPASS shows you exactly what to change there. **You stay on your current ship until db.sfi.org shows the change**, and then COMPASS moves you automatically.

**Transfer History** on My Record lists every move you've made.

## Common Issues

**I never got a welcome email, or the link expired.**
Use **Forgot password?** on the login page with your SFI username or email. If COMPASS doesn't recognise you, ask your CO to check that you're on the ship's roster.

**My rank, name or email in COMPASS is wrong.**
These come from db.sfi.org. Get them corrected there first; COMPASS picks up the change the next time your CO imports the roster.

**My membership expiration or OTS/OCC status is out of date.**
Click **Sync My SFI Data** on My Record or your Profile.

**An event I attended isn't on my record.**
Attendance is entered by your command team after the event, and only approved events count. If it's still missing a few days later, let your CO know.

**My permanent points total is lower than the points I've been awarded.**
Some categories are capped — see [Academy and Permanent Points](#academy-and-permanent-points).

**I'm not receiving notifications.**
Check your **Email Notification Preferences** on your Profile and your spam folder. For push notifications, click **Enable on this Device** on each device, and make sure your browser allows notifications for compass.sfi.org.
